Privacy Policy

Postiz Content Publisher is a private, self-hosted publishing tool used by its owner.

When the owner connects a TikTok account, the service receives authorization tokens and basic account information needed to publish content selected by the owner.

This information is used only to provide scheduling and publishing functionality for the connected account. It is not sold, rented, or shared for advertising.

Authorization information is stored on the owner’s self-hosted system and can be revoked by removing the connected channel or revoking the app from TikTok account settings.

To request deletion or ask a privacy question, contact: nova.the.admin@gmail.com
